Biometric devices

Connect the office terminals so every punch lands in Attendance next to Virtual Office clock-ins.

Three ways to connect, whatever the brand

Cloud push

The device sends each punch straight to the Virtual Office over the internet. Most modern terminals support this (often called ADMS, Cloud Server or HTTP push).

Real time. Recommended.

Office sync agent

A small program on one office PC reads the devices over the office network every minute and uploads new punches. For older units without cloud push.

About 1 minute behind.

File import

Export the attendance log from the device (USB stick or its software) and upload it here. Works with any brand as a fallback.

Manual. Duplicates are skipped.
